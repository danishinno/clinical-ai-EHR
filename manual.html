Health Sync EHR

Clinical Scribe Operations Manual

Medical Officer

Introduction

Health Sync leverages advanced AI-driven audio processing and semantic clinical guidance to transform patient interactions. This guide covers how to effectively operate the system to ensure smooth consultations and compliant clinical operations.

System Workflow Instructions

  • 1
    Queue & Demographics Banner
    Toggle the Queue Sidebar to see patients currently waiting on hold. Click on a patient to load their clinical profile. Their basic details and medical alerts (allergies, vitals) will instantly populate the horizontal demographics banner at the top of the workspace.
  • 2
    Ambient Live Dictation
    When the patient is ready, click "Start Live Dictation". The AI listening engine will capture clinical speech. Speak naturally during the interview. The system displays a live transcript as you talk.
  • 3
    Structured SOAP Extraction
    Click "End Consultation" when the clinical interview is complete. The system will process the transcription and automatically extract a structured clinical record organized by Subjective (S), Objective (O), Assessment (A), and Plan (P).
  • 4
    Review, Edit & Sign Notes
    Click in any SOAP data field (e.g. Assessment or Plan) to edit the details directly. Ensure all diagnoses and prescriptions are correct. When finalized, click "Sign & Save" to apply a digital cryptographic signature stamp to the note, locking it against future modification.
  • 5
    Clinical Brain CDS (Clinical Decision Support)
    Access evidence-based guidelines on-demand by clicking the floating "Brain Logo FAB" in the bottom right corner. Ask clinical questions or request diagnostic checklists based directly on the uploaded medical guidelines database.
⚠️ Medical Certificate (MC) Compliance Restrictions

Medical certificates are legal documents subject to strict compliance rules. The system enforces the following logic to prevent retroactive changes and unauthorized issuances:

  • No Past-Day Certificates: Medical certificates cannot be generated for consultations completed on previous calendar days.
  • 2-Hour Issuance Limit: A Medical Certificate must be generated within 2 hours (120 minutes) of the consultation end time (when the doctor clicked "Finish"). After 2 hours, the "Generate MC" button will be permanently disabled.
  • Prompt Issuance Advice: Ensure you review, edit, and issue necessary MCs immediately after ending the consultation to avoid lockout.

Health Sync EHR

Administration Operations Manual

System Administrator

Introduction

Welcome to the Health Sync Administration system. As an administrator, you manage doctor approvals, clinical guidelines, patient queuing operations, and clinic performance report analytics.

Administrative Workflows

  • 1
    Doctor Approvals
    All newly registered doctors are held in a pending state until verified. Review credentials in the "Pending Doctor Approvals" list and click "Approve" to activate their account and grant dashboard access.
  • 2
    Clinical Guidelines Storage & Display
    Maintain the medical database by uploading clinical PDF guidelines. The system automatically parses these files and records them in the local guidelines database. The uploaded guidelines are displayed in the "Current Clinical Guidelines" table for audit tracking.
  • 3
    Patient Queue Management
    Register new patients or retrieve returning patients from the database. Assign patients to specific doctors. Admins can view the live consultation queue and re-assign patients to a different doctor if needed.
  • 4
    Clinic Performance & Analytics Reports
    The system tracks clinic-wide consultation counts and medication prescriptions. Use the "Monthly Clinic Analytics & Reports" module to generate monthly reports outlining total visits, total prescription counts, top prescribed drugs, consultation breakdowns by doctor, and daily clinic activity.
